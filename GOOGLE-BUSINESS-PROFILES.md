## REVIEW GENERATION SYSTEM

### The Process (Same for All Three)

1. **Complete the job** — make sure customer is happy
2. **Same day (2-4 hours later)** — Send WhatsApp:
   > "Hi [name]! Glad we got the [specific job] sorted for you today. If you've got 30 seconds, a quick Google review would really help us out — here's the link: [direct review link]. No worries if not, cheers!"
3. **Day 3** — If no review, follow up:
   > "Hey [name], just checking the [car] is running well after the [job]. If you did get a chance to leave a review that'd be great — [link]. Either way, hope it's all good! 👍"
4. **Log it** — Track who you've asked, who's reviewed, conversion rate

### Getting the Direct Review Link

For each profile:
1. Search your business name on Google
2. Click "Write a review"
3. Copy that URL — OR use: `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_PLACE_ID`

### What Makes a Perfect Review (Coach Customers)

A killer review for local SEO contains:
- **The specific service** ("timing belt replacement")
- **The location** ("came to my house in Digmoor")
- **The experience** ("on time, fair price, done in 3 hours")
- **A recommendation** ("would recommend to anyone in Skem")

You can't dictate what people write, but you CAN say things like:
> "If you could mention what we did and where we came to, that really helps other people find us — cheers!"

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. ❌ **Don't use the same photos** on multiple profiles — Google can detect duplicates
2. ❌ **Don't post on the same days** — vary the schedule
3. ❌ **Don't use identical descriptions** — each must be unique
4. ❌ **Don't link profiles to each other** (no cross-linking)
5. ❌ **Don't use the same Gmail account** for multiple profiles
6. ❌ **Don't keyword-stuff the business name** — "Skem Mobile Mechanics" is fine, "Skem Mobile Mechanics Cheap Brakes Skelmersdale" will get suspended
7. ❌ **Don't add a physical address if you're mobile** — use service area only
8. ❌ **Don't let reviews go unanswered** — every single one gets a response
9. ❌ **Don't stop posting** — consistency beats volume
10. ❌ **Don't ignore the Q&A section** — competitors can post misleading questions
