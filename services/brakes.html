Signs your brakes need attention

Brakes give clear warning signs before they fail completely. Ignoring them doesn't just wear the components faster — it compromises your safety and can turn a £65 pad change into a £300+ caliper and disc replacement.

Symptoms of brake wear

  • Squealing or screeching — the wear indicator tab is contacting the disc surface
  • Grinding or metallic scraping — pads are worn through to the backing plate, disc damage is occurring
  • Vibration or pulsing through the pedal — warped or excessively worn discs
  • Pulling to one side under braking — seized caliper or uneven pad wear
  • Spongy pedal or excessive travel — air in the system or worn master cylinder
  • Handbrake not holding or excessive lever travel
  • Brake warning light illuminated (fluid level or pad wear sensor)

Why pad quality matters

Cheap brake pads are a false economy. Budget pads squeal constantly, produce excessive brake dust that corrodes alloy wheels, wear discs prematurely, and have inferior stopping performance — particularly when hot. Common questions

About brake repair in Skelmersdale.

How do I know my brakes need doing?

Squealing is the early warning — that's the wear indicator doing its job. Grinding means you're past due and the backing plate is damaging the disc. Vibration through the pedal indicates warped discs. Any of these mean it's time. We offer free visual brake checks if you're unsure.

Do you replace pads and discs together?

If the discs are below minimum thickness, scored, or lipped, they need replacing with the pads. If they're still within spec and in good condition, pads alone are sufficient. We'll measure and advise — we don't replace components that don't need it.

What brand brake pads do you use?

Pagid, Brembo, TRW, or Mintex depending on the application. All ECE R90-approved, all engineered for the specific vehicle. Not eBay specials that squeal after a week and eat your discs.

How long do brakes last?

Front pads typically last 25,000-50,000 miles depending on driving style and vehicle weight. Rears last longer — usually 40,000-70,000 miles. Discs generally last two sets of pads. Stop-start city driving wears them faster than motorway cruising.
