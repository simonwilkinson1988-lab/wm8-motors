Servicing done properly

A service is only as good as the oil and filters that go in and the inspection that happens around it. We use manufacturer-specification oil (correct viscosity and approvals for your engine) and OE-quality filters — not the cheapest items from a motor factor clearance shelf. The difference in longevity and engine protection is significant.

Why mobile servicing works

No sitting in a waiting room watching daytime television. No leaving your car at a garage and arranging lifts. We arrive at your booked time slot, service the vehicle on your driveway or at your workplace, and you carry on with your day. The car never leaves your sight — you know exactly what's been done.

Quality matters

The difference between a £30 service and a proper one is the oil and filters. Cheap mineral oil in an engine that requires fully synthetic long-life spec will cause accelerated wear, sludge build-up, and eventually expensive failures. We use the correct specification for your engine — always. Castrol, Mobil 1, Fuchs, Total — whichever carries the right manufacturer approval for your vehicle.

Common questions

About car servicing in Skelmersdale.

What's included in a full service?

Oil and filter, air filter, cabin filter, spark plugs or fuel filter where due, all fluid checks and top-ups, brake inspection, tyre check, underside inspection, diagnostic scan, and service book stamp. Everything your manufacturer recommends at the service interval.

Do you stamp the service book?

Yes. We stamp your physical service book and can also provide a digital service record. Manufacturer warranties are not affected by independent servicing provided the correct specification parts and fluids are used — which they always are.

What oil do you use?

The correct specification for your engine — always. This means fully synthetic long-life where required (VW 504/507, BMW LL-04, MB 229.51, etc.) and conventional or semi-synthetic where that's what the manufacturer specifies. We don't use one-size-fits-all oil.

How often should I service my car?

Most manufacturers recommend annually or every 10,000-12,000 miles, whichever comes first. Some specify longer intervals (up to 20,000 miles) but we recommend interim services between these for engine longevity — especially on turbocharged diesels where oil degradation is accelerated.
