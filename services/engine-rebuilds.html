Top end, bottom end, and full engine rebuilds. Methodical strip-down, precision machining, and reassembly to factory torque specifications with new bearings, rings, and seals throughout.

When an engine needs rebuilding

An engine rebuild becomes necessary when internal components have worn beyond service limits or suffered catastrophic failure. It's a significant undertaking, but on the right vehicle it's considerably more cost-effective than replacement — and you end up with an engine you know the history of, built to spec with new internals.

Signs you need a rebuild

  • Bottom end knock — deep metallic knocking from the crankshaft area, worse under load
  • Excessive oil consumption — burning more than 1 litre per 1000 miles with no external leaks
  • Low compression across multiple cylinders — engine lacks power and runs rough
  • Blue smoke on startup or under acceleration — worn valve stems or piston rings
  • Catastrophic failure — timing belt/chain failure, hydraulic lock, oil starvation
  • Bore scoring or glazing visible on inspection
  • Metal contamination in the oil (bearing material)

Types of rebuild

Not every engine issue requires a full strip. We assess what's needed and quote accordingly:

  • Top end rebuild — Cylinder head removal, valve stem seals, valve lapping/replacement, head gasket, head skim. Addresses oil burning and compression loss from valve issues. From £800.
  • Bottom end rebuild — Crankshaft bearings (big end and main), connecting rods, oil pump, seals. Addresses knocking, low oil pressure, bearing failure. From £1200.
  • Full rebuild — Complete strip to bare block. Re-bore or hone cylinders, new pistons and rings, bearings throughout, all seals and gaskets, timing components. The engine is returned to as-new specification. From £2500.

What's involved

  • Complete engine strip-down and inspection of all components
  • Crankshaft measurement — journals checked for ovality and taper
  • Cylinder bore measurement — checked for wear, taper, and ovality
  • Machine work — bore honing or re-boring, crankshaft grinding where needed
  • New piston rings, bearings (big end and main), oil seals throughout
  • Cylinder head overhaul — valves, seats, guides, springs
  • New timing kit, oil pump, water pump where applicable
  • Assembly to factory torque specifications with calibrated equipment
  • Oil priming, run-in procedure, and post-build inspection

How much does it cost?

Engine rebuild costs vary significantly depending on the scope of work:

  • Top end rebuild: from £800
  • Bottom end rebuild: from £1200
  • Full rebuild: from £2500+

We provide a fixed price after initial inspection. The engine needs to be stripped and measured before we can quote accurately — there's no way to price a rebuild without seeing what's inside. Initial assessment and strip-down is quoted separately.

How long does it take?

A top end rebuild typically takes 2-4 days. Bottom end work takes 3-5 days. A full rebuild with machine shop work can take 1-2 weeks depending on parts availability and machining lead times. We'll keep you updated throughout.

Common questions

About engine rebuilds in Skelmersdale.

How do I know if my engine needs rebuilding?

Persistent bottom end knock, excessive oil consumption (more than 1L per 1000 miles), low compression readings, or metal contamination in the oil all point towards rebuild territory. We can diagnose definitively with compression testing, oil analysis, and bore scope inspection.

Is it cheaper to buy a second-hand engine?

Sometimes — but you're buying an unknown quantity. Second-hand engines come with someone else's neglect. A rebuild gives you known tolerances, new bearings, fresh seals, and a defined lifespan. For vehicles worth keeping, a rebuild is the superior option. For older low-value cars, a used engine may make more financial sense.

How long does a rebuild take?

Top end: 2-4 days. Bottom end: 3-5 days. Full rebuild: 1-2 weeks depending on machine shop lead times and parts availability. We don't rush precision work — it's built right or it's not built at all.

Do you do engine swaps?

Yes. Where a rebuild isn't economically viable, we can source and fit a replacement engine — used, recon, or new. We handle the removal, swap, and all ancillary connections including wiring, cooling, and exhaust.
