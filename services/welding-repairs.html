Welding Repairs & Sill Replacement in Skelmersdale

Structural welding, sill repairs, chassis patching, and MOT corrosion failures — sorted at your door with a proper MIG setup. The work most mechanics won't touch.

Why welding work is hard to find locally

Most mobile mechanics don't carry welding equipment. Most garages either don't have a skilled welder on staff or charge bodyshop rates that make the repair uneconomical. That leaves a lot of people being told their car is scrap when it's actually a few hours of welding away from passing an MOT.

We've been carrying out structural welding and fabrication repairs across Skelmersdale and the WN8 area for over 20 years. We bring a full MIG welding rig, grinders, panel fabrication tools, and everything needed to carry out proper structural metalwork repairs on your driveway or at your workplace.

Sill repairs and replacement

Sills are the most common MOT welding failure we deal with. They corrode from the inside out — often looking fine on the surface until the tester pushes a screwdriver through them. Around Skelmersdale, the combination of salted winter roads and standing water accelerates this massively, especially on older vehicles.

We carry out:

  • Patch repairs to localised corrosion
  • Full sill panel replacement (inner and outer)
  • Sill closing plates and strengthening sections
  • Jacking point reconstruction and reinforcement
  • Sill-to-floor pan junction repairs

A full sill replacement typically takes 3-5 hours per side depending on the extent of the corrosion and whether it's spread into adjacent panels. We cut back to solid metal, fabricate or fit replacement sections, and finish with rust protection and underseal.

MOT structural failure repairs

When the MOT tester flags structural corrosion, it needs welding — there's no bodge that'll pass a retest. Common MOT welding failures we repair include:

  • Sill corrosion (inner, outer, or both)
  • Wheel arch lip rot and structural corrosion
  • Chassis rail surface rust and perforation
  • Subframe mounting point corrosion
  • Spring hanger and outrigger rot
  • Floor pan perforation (driver's side, rear footwells)
  • Brake line mounting corrosion
  • Seat belt mounting point structural integrity

We'll look at your MOT fail sheet, assess the actual extent of the damage (it's sometimes less than you think), and give you a fixed price to get it through the retest. No guesswork, no open-ended labour charges.

Chassis and structural welding

Beyond MOT work, we carry out structural welding on chassis components that garages won't touch:

  • Chassis rail section replacement
  • Subframe repairs and reinforcement
  • Crossmember patching
  • Towing eye and recovery point repairs
  • Suspension turret reinforcement
  • Mounting bracket fabrication

Exhaust welding and custom fabrication

Rather than replacing an entire exhaust system because one section has blown, we can weld a repair section in place or fabricate custom pipe work. Particularly useful for older vehicles where pattern exhausts are no longer available, or performance setups that need bespoke sections welded to fit.

How much does welding repair cost?

Welding repairs in Skelmersdale typically range from £80 for a small patch to £300-500 for full sill replacements. Chassis rail work and complex multi-area repairs are quoted individually based on scope. We'll always give you a fixed price after inspecting the vehicle — what we quote is what you pay.

Our approach

We don't just slap filler over rust and hope for the best. Every welding repair follows the same process:

  • Cut back corroded metal until solid material is reached
  • Fabricate or fit OE-spec replacement panels
  • MIG weld with proper penetration (not tack and pray)
  • Grind, dress, and finish welds cleanly
  • Apply weld-through primer to all joints
  • Underseal and cavity wax to prevent future corrosion

Common questions

About welding repairs and sill replacement in Skelmersdale.

Can you actually weld on a driveway?

Yes. We carry a full MIG welding rig with its own power supply, plus grinders, cutting tools, and panel fabrication equipment. We set up properly with fire blankets and appropriate shielding. It's what we do every week.

My car failed its MOT on corrosion — is it worth repairing?

Usually, yes. Most structural corrosion failures cost £80-£400 to repair properly. If the rest of the car is mechanically sound, it's almost always worth it. We'll give you an honest assessment — if it's genuinely beyond economic repair, we'll tell you straight.

How long does a sill repair take?

A patch repair on one area takes 1-2 hours. A full sill replacement (one side) takes 3-5 hours depending on how far the rot has spread. We'll confirm timescale when we inspect and quote.

Will it pass the MOT retest after your repair?

Yes. We repair to MOT structural standard — proper metal thickness, adequate penetration, and sound construction. We know exactly what the tester is looking for because we see these fail sheets every week. If we say it'll pass, it'll pass.

Do you repair wheel arches and bodywork rust?

We handle structural wheel arch corrosion (the stuff that fails MOTs). For cosmetic bodywork and resprays, you'd need a body shop — but we can sort the metal underneath so it's structurally sound before any cosmetic work is done.
